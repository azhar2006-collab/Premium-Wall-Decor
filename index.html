Our Heritage

MORE THAN DECOR. IT'S A PASSION FOR TWO WHEELS.

KINETIC MOTO was born in the intersection between high-performance motorcycle engineering and contemporary fine art. As lifelong riders and design artisans, we grew frustrated that miniature models were relegated to dusty shelves or flat 2D posters that failed to convey mechanical visceral power.

We spent three years perfecting our signature architectural shadowbox architecture. By suspending precision 1:12 scale three-dimensional motorcycle sculptures in high relief against anti-glare carbon fiber, we turn legendary machines into permanent, conversation-starting wall centerpieces.

Every single piece is hand-assembled, individually numbered, and checked against real motorcycle blueprints before sealing behind museum-grade acrylic.
